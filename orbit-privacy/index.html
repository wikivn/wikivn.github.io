Orbit for Android

Privacy Policy

Orbit works entirely on your Android device. This policy explains what the app accesses, why it needs that information, and the choices available to you.

Effective: July 31, 2026 Application ID: co.bubbles Publisher: wikivn
No network permission Orbit cannot send anything anywhere. There is no server behind it.
No ads or analytics No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking software.
On-device processing App information and preferences never leave your device.

1. Overview

Orbit (“the App”) is provided by wikivn (“we”, “us”, or “the developer”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, where it is stored, and the choices available to you.

Orbit does not request the INTERNET permission. It has no technical means of sending data off your device, and we do not operate a server for it. We do not collect, receive, or sell your personal information.

Android’s backup and device-transfer services may copy the limited preferences described below to your own backup account or another device. The developer cannot access those backups.

2. Information the App accesses

Installed and launchable apps

Orbit reads the list of apps on your device that have a launcher activity. For each visible app, Orbit accesses:

  • the app name;
  • the package identifier;
  • the launcher activity; and
  • the app icon.

Orbit uses this information to display and search your app list, let you choose apps for Quick launch, open apps through Android Bubbles, and create local notifications and launcher shortcuts.

This list is read and used on your device only. Orbit does not request the QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission; it declares a <queries> element that grants visibility of launchable apps and nothing more.

Preferences stored on your device

Orbit stores only the preferences needed to provide its features:

  • package identifiers for apps you add to Quick launch;
  • package identifiers for apps you hide;
  • your Quick launch notification mode;
  • your light, dark, or system theme choice; and
  • whether you dismissed the in-app usage tip.

These are kept in Orbit’s own private app storage, which other apps cannot read. Your per-app language selection is not stored by Orbit — it is managed by Android’s own system language service.

3. Information the App does not access

Orbit does not access, collect, or process:

  • your searches, usage history, or which apps you open;
  • the contents of notifications from other apps — Orbit does not request or use Android’s notification-listener access;
  • your contacts, messages, call logs, location, photos, files, camera, or microphone;
  • advertising identifiers, or any account information.

4. Advertising and analytics

Orbit contains no advertising and no analytics. There is no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no crash-reporting SDK in the App, and the App has no network permission with which to contact one.

5. Android notifications, Bubbles, and launcher shortcuts

Orbit uses Android system services to show the Quick launch notification, create Bubbles, and publish launcher shortcuts. App names, icons, and package identifiers are provided to Android system components on the same device, only as needed to render and operate those features.

The App requests the POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission on supported Android versions. You can deny or revoke it in Android Settings, though Quick launch and Bubbles will not work without it.

6. Links that leave the App

Orbit’s Settings screen contains a link to this Privacy Policy. Opening it hands the web address to whichever browser you have chosen; the request is made by that browser, under its own privacy policy, not by Orbit.

7. Backup and device transfer

If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may back up the preferences listed above, including package identifiers for apps you added to Quick launch or hid. Android may also transfer those preferences directly to a new device.

These operations are controlled by Android and your backup provider. Backup data is associated with your own device or backup account, is not sent to the developer, and cannot be accessed by the developer. You can manage or disable backup through your device or account settings.

8. Data sharing and sale

Orbit does not sell user data and does not share it with third parties. The developer does not receive your installed-app list, your searches, your Quick launch selections, or your hidden-app selections. No third-party service receives them either.

Android platform services process this information locally on your device, or provide the backup and device-transfer functionality described above.

9. Data retention and deletion

Preferences remain on your device until you:

  • remove or change them in the App;
  • clear Orbit’s storage in Android Settings; or
  • uninstall the App.

Backups previously created by Android may remain in your backup account according to the backup provider’s retention rules. You can manage or delete those backups through your device or account settings.

The developer does not hold your installed-app list or your Orbit preferences, and so has nothing to delete on request.

10. Security

Orbit relies on Android’s application sandbox and permission model to protect its local data. Because the App has no network permission, none of that data leaves your device. No storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

11. Children’s privacy

Orbit is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s features or data practices change. The revised policy will show a new effective date. Material changes will be reflected in the App or its Google Play listing as appropriate.
