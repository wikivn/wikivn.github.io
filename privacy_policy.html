Orbit for Android

Privacy Policy

Orbit is designed to work locally on your Android device. This policy explains what the app accesses, why it needs that information, and the choices available to you.

Effective: July 28, 2026 Application ID: co.bubbles Publisher: wikivn
On-device processing App information and preferences stay on your device.
Google-served ads One banner is shown inside Orbit, subject to your privacy choices.
No developer server Your app list and Orbit preferences are not sent to us.
No data sale We do not sell your personal information.

1. Overview

Orbit (“the App”) is provided by wikivn (“we”, “us”, or “the developer”). Orbit’s launcher features work locally, and we do not operate a server that receives your installed-app list or Orbit preferences.

The App uses Google Mobile Ads to display a banner advertisement. Google may collect and share limited device, interaction, diagnostic, and identifier data for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention as described below.

2. Information the App accesses

Installed and launchable apps

Orbit reads the apps on your device that have a launcher activity. For each visible app, Orbit may access:

  • the app name and icon;
  • the package identifier; and
  • the launcher activity.

This information is used only on your device to display and search your app list, let you choose apps for Quick Launch, open apps through Android Bubbles, and create local notifications and launcher shortcuts.

The list of launchable apps is not sent to us or a remote server. Orbit does not request the QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission.

3. Preferences stored on your device

Orbit stores only the preferences needed to provide its features:

  • package identifiers for apps you add to Quick Launch;
  • package identifiers for apps you hide;
  • your Quick Launch notification mode;
  • your light, dark, or system theme choice; and
  • whether you dismissed the in-app usage tip.

Your per-app language selection is managed by Android’s system language service. Orbit’s launcher features do not store your searches, usage history, contacts, messages, location, photos, files, advertising identifiers, or account information. Data processed by Google’s advertising SDK is described below.

4. Advertising and privacy choices

Orbit uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display one banner advertisement inside the App. Orbit does not display ads in Android notifications, Bubbles, Quick Launch, other apps, or elsewhere on your device.

According to Google’s SDK disclosure, Google Mobile Ads automatically collects and shares the following data for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • App and advertisement interactions, including launches, taps, impressions, and video views;
  • diagnostic information, including app or SDK performance, hang rate, and energy usage; and
  • device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

Google states that this data is encrypted in transit and handles it under Google’s Privacy Policy.

Orbit uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to request consent or present privacy choices where required. When available, you can change these choices from Settings → Privacy → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete the Android advertising ID in Android Settings.

5. Notifications, Bubbles, and shortcuts

Orbit uses Android system services to show the Quick Launch notification, create Bubbles, and publish launcher shortcuts. App names, icons, and package identifiers may be provided to Android system components on the same device only as needed to operate those features.

The App requests the POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission on supported Android versions. You may deny or revoke this permission in Android Settings, but Quick Launch and Bubble features will not work without notifications.

6. Android backup and device transfer

If Android backup is enabled, Android may back up Orbit’s stored preferences, including package identifiers for apps you added to Quick Launch or hid. Android may also transfer those preferences directly to a new device.

These operations are controlled by Android and your backup provider. Backup data is associated with your own device or backup account. It is not sent to us, and we cannot access it. You can manage or disable backup through your device or account settings.

7. Data sharing and sale

Orbit does not sell user data. We do not receive your installed-app list, searches, Quick Launch selections, or hidden-app selections.

Google Mobile Ads collects and shares advertising-related data with Google and its advertising partners as described above. Google may provide us with aggregated advertising reports. Android platform services also process information locally, or provide backup and device-transfer functionality.

8. Data retention and deletion

Preferences remain on your device until you:

  • remove or change them in the App;
  • clear Orbit’s storage in Android Settings; or
  • uninstall the App.

Backups previously created by Android may remain in your backup account according to your backup provider’s retention rules. You can manage or delete those backups through your device or account settings.

We do not hold your installed-app list or Orbit preferences. Google retains advertising-related data according to its own policies. You can change available advertising privacy choices from Orbit’s Settings and manage your advertising ID in Android Settings.

9. Security

Orbit relies on Android’s application sandbox and system permission model to protect its local data. Advertising-related network traffic is handled by Google Mobile Ads, which states that collected data is encrypted in transit. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Children’s privacy

Orbit is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s features or data practices change. The revised policy will show a new effective date. Material changes will be reflected in the App or its Google Play listing as appropriate.
