1. About this policy

Stickio (application ID co.stickers) is a sticker creation and animation app provided by wikivn. This policy explains how we handle information when you use the app and contact us.

No Stickio account is required. We operate no server for your creations. Photos, drawings, stickers, boards and Studio projects are processed on your device. Google services, Android backup and apps you choose to share with may handle information as explained below.

2. Photos, camera and creations

Stickio accesses photos you select through Android’s photo picker and pictures you take with the optional in-app camera. It uses them to cut out subjects, make stickers, edit drawings, assemble collages and animations, and create wallpapers. It does not request broad access to your photo library.

Your saved content, drafts, sticker packs, board layouts, tags and preferences are stored in the app’s local storage. Temporary files support editing and sharing. Stickio does not upload your photos, camera images or creations to the developer.

3. Permissions and device features

  • Camera: requested when you use the camera. You can deny or revoke it in Android Settings and still select existing photos.
  • Motion sensors: gravity and acceleration readings animate stickers as you tilt or shake the device. These readings are processed locally and are not stored as a movement history. You can switch tilt off in Settings.
  • Vibration: provides touch feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • Live wallpaper: Android displays the board or Studio scene you choose on your home or lock screen. Anyone who can see that screen may see its content.

Google Play services and included libraries may use network access for purchases, model downloads, fonts and technical diagnostics. Stickio does not request microphone, contacts, location or message access.

4. Automatic cutouts and Google ML Kit

Automatic subject cutouts use Google ML Kit. Image analysis and its results stay on your device; ML Kit does not send those images or results to Google servers.

ML Kit may connect to Google for model downloads, updates and compatibility information. It also sends technical diagnostics and usage metrics, including device and app details, identifiers, timing, errors and API configuration such as image dimensions. Google handles this data under its own policy; the developer does not receive it. See ML Kit privacy information, ML Kit data disclosures and Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. Stickio Pro and payments

Subscriptions and one-time Pro purchases are processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment method and billing information; Stickio does not receive your card details. The app requests product and purchase information, acknowledges completed purchases, and remembers Pro status locally to provide paid features.

At checkout, Stickio sends Google Play a randomly generated installation identifier as an obfuscated account ID to help prevent purchase misuse. This is not a Stickio login and does not contain your name or email address. We do not run a purchase-verification server. Google retains purchase records under its own policies. Clearing app data or uninstalling does not cancel a subscription; manage it in Google Play.

6. Downloaded fonts

Some Pro fonts are downloaded through the Google Fonts provider in Google Play services. Stickio requests the font family and style; it does not send the text you type or your artwork with the request. Google handles downloads and associated service data under Google’s Privacy Policy.

7. Exporting and sharing

When you save an export, Stickio writes the selected image, video or GIF to a destination on your device. When you share, the app you choose receives access to the exported file. Adding a sticker pack to WhatsApp or Telegram gives that service the pack content and relevant metadata, such as its name and publisher.

These actions happen at your request. The receiving app’s policies apply, including WhatsApp’s Privacy Policy and Telegram’s Privacy Policy. Removing a creation from Stickio does not remove copies you exported, sent or added to another app.

8. Support and external links

If you send feedback, your email app opens a draft with the app name and version, device manufacturer and model, and Android version. You can edit the draft before sending. We receive your email address, message and any attachments only if you send them, and use them to respond and resolve your request.

Rating, sharing, subscription management and privacy links open the relevant store, app or browser. This policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose hosting service may process request information such as your IP address and browser details under GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

9. Advertising, analytics and sale of data

Stickio contains no advertising SDK, separate analytics SDK or crash-reporting SDK. We do not sell personal information or use your creations for advertising. Google ML Kit’s technical metrics and the other service interactions described above still apply.

10. Android backup and device transfer

Android backup is enabled. Depending on your device, backup settings and platform limits, Android may copy eligible app files, databases and preferences to your backup account or another device. This may include creations, settings and locally stored Pro information. We cannot access those backups.

You control backup and device transfer through Android and your backup provider. Their policies govern retention and deletion; restoring a backup may restore previously saved app data.

11. Retention and deletion

Local content remains until you delete it in the app, clear Stickio’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Temporary files may remain until cleaned up by the app or Android. Exported files, shared copies and system backups must be deleted separately in the relevant location or service.

We keep support correspondence only as needed to handle your request and follow-up, or meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion of correspondence using the contact below. Google and other providers control retention of their service and purchase records; the developer cannot delete those records on their behalf. There is no Stickio account to delete.

12. Security and children’s privacy

Stickio relies on Android’s app sandbox and permission controls to protect local data. Share and export only content you intend others to see. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through support correspondence. If you believe a child sent us personal information, contact us so we can address it and delete it as appropriate. Google Play applies its own purchase and parental controls.

13. Policy updates

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be available at this address, linked from Settings, with a revised effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent.
